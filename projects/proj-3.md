title: 'Influence of the urinary microbiota on high risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer and BCG immunotherapy'
---

BCG immunotherapy is commonly used to prevent bladder cancer recurrence, yet it fails in 30–50% of patients, often with notable side effects. This study analyzed the urinary microbiota of 47 male NMIBC patients,combining 16S rRNA gene sequencing with real-time quantitative PCR (qPCR) targetting the 16S rRNA genes, before their first and last BCG instillations to investigate whether microbial profiles were associated with treatment outcome. 

This project is in collaboration with Dr. Denise Nardelli Haefliger, PhD and Dr. Laurent DERRÉ, PhD from the Urology Research Unit and Urology Biobank, Deptment of Urology, Lausanne University Hospital (CHUV)
